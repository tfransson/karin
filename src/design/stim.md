*\- Floating movements in hand-blown glass. A colleciton of vessels inspired by the sea.*

##### Brief
Design and make a wooden mould for glassblowing to bring to Skruf Glassworks. 

##### Inspiration
I seek inspiration from the origin of the material. To view the bottom of the ocean is a sight of movement and light.

##### A collection 
The design is developed with the technique in mind. The collection of vessels are all made from the same mould. The different cutting heights and a reversable shape creates a family of glass.

##### Mould making
The mould is drawn in fusion360 and made in a CNC mill. The last steps of carving, drilling holes, burning the wood and assembly are made by hand.

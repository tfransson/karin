##### Brief
With digital tools. Devolop a dining table with matching seating for outdoor use in pine wood. Stackable, foldable or knock-down.

##### Ideation
The foldable furniture have something lively and interactive to it. The combination of textile and wood was something I wanted to explore. It´s a clever and lightweight solution. 

##### Pattern
The pattern of the textile is inspired by Shibori, a japanese technique where fabric is folded and dyed. The expression reflects the concept of the furniture, to fold. 

##### Final design
The emphasis of this project has been on construction and function. The result is a foldable set of furniture. The table and chair are following the same design principles, to create a coherent expression, despite different materials.
